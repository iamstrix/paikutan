Trumpo Priorities

Ordered by impact per token. Submission deadline: October 25, 2026.

Tier 1: cheap to build, high impact (done)

  1. Faster pace and more responsive movement (done: median round went from 16.2s to 11.8s)
  2. Characters cosmetic only (done)
  3. Trumpo stat trade-offs (done: 9-point budget per trumpo)
  4. Arrow guideline for steering (done)
  5. More oomph on power-ups (done: shockwaves, flashes, shake, particles)
  6. Win animation (done: winner close-up, confetti, fireworks for the match winner)
  7. B-roll cameras (done: C cycles camera, H hides HUD)

Added after Tier 1 (done)

  • Dash for every trumpo (done: Shift/Q or DASH button, 2.5s cooldown, costs 4% spin, no extra weight)
  • Own steering arrow drawn from local input (done: no host round trip, stays visible, dims when not steering)
  • Comeback: power charges up to 1.5x faster when behind on spin (done)
  • Comeback: agaw-ikot, dash into the leader while behind to steal spin (done, needs a human playtest to judge)

Tier 2: moderate cost, high impact

  1. Filipino street look: yero roofs and tindahan stalls built from simple shapes and textures
  2. Tricycle hazard: one moving object on a timer that knocks trumpos aside
  3. Menu redesign
  4. Signature character powers, so characters have an identity without stats
  5. Power pacing and balance pass (watch round length: dash and agaw-ikot brought the CPU median down to about 7s)
  6. Shrinking circle ("Sikip na!"): after 35s the chalk circle closes from radius 5 to 3 over 20s, replacing the double spin drain, so nobody can run down the clock
  7. Tikbalang Stampede: replaces the straight charge. About 2.5s of double weight and higher top speed while you keep steering, hits that ignore most of Agimat's shield, and a hoof-stomp shockwave at the end
  8. Optional: last-spin mode below 25% spin (glow, heavier hits). Only if agaw-ikot is not enough

Tier 3: expensive, strong for finals and esports scoring

  1. Throw-motion gesture, or throw then pull
  2. Tikbalang charge on a separate joystick
  3. Free-for-all with n trumpos (streams, giveaways)
  4. Phone POV camera
  5. Dog hazard upgrade (animation and pathing)
