Paikutan sound effect audit

Sound effect references for each trumpo. Compiled 2026-10-25.

Current state

  • The game has no audio files. Every sound is synthesized in code in js/audio.js.
  • Kidlat, Agimat, Diwata and Bakunawa each have their own synthesized power sound.
  • Tikbalang has no sound of its own. Its power reuses the generic dash sound. The stampede's end stomp has its own thud.

Licensing

  • Every candidate below came from a Freesound search filtered to CC0, which means free to use, even commercially, with no credit required.
  • Only the T'boli kulintang page was opened and its CC0 license confirmed. Check the license on each sound's page before downloading.
  • None of the candidates has been listened to yet.

Candidates per trumpo

  • LoudThunder with crack and Sizzle by kvgarlic

Tikbalang (Tikbalang Stampede)

Target sound: a pounding gallop for the 2 s stampede, ending in the stomp. This is the only trumpo with no power sound of its own today.

  • 65 - Galloping horse by GiordanoFabricio
  • HorseGallop1.wav by gbnelso
  • cartoon horse gallop.wav by martian (a more playful option)

Gap: no clean CC0 horse neigh was found.

Agimat (Amulet Shield)

Target sound: a bright metallic ward, a struck bronze tone with a shimmer.

Diwata (Diwata's Blessing)

Target sound: bamboo and air, gentle and rising.

Gap: no CC0 kubing (Philippine bamboo jaw harp) recording was found.

Bakunawa (Eclipse)

Target sound: a deep serpent growl, answered by gongs.

  • T'boli Tribe South Cotabato by Peligro8. A kulintang recording from Mindanao with lake ambience, 2:24 long, license confirmed CC0.
  • Deep Monster Growl by uagadugu
  • Long howl) whale and monster.wav by J.R.Mythical
  • Temple Gong 2 by kerozenne

This is the strongest pick for the theme. In the folklore, villagers bang pots and gongs during an eclipse to scare the Bakunawa into spitting the moon back out. A growl followed by a burst of kulintang tells that story with a real Mindanao recording. Cut a few strikes from the 2:24 clip. Credit it as T'boli music even though CC0 does not require it.

Spin hum (all trumpos)

Target sound: the actual top spinning.

  • SpinningTop.wav by gazbooth
  • Metallic Spinning Top (Dry Sound).wav by Uzbazur

These are reference only. The current hum is synthesized and follows spin speed live, which a recording cannot do.

Recommended approach

  1. Use real recordings for the five power moments only. Trim each to 1 to 2 seconds and save it as a small mp3, about 150 KB for all five. This matters for phones on mobile data.
  2. Keep the synthesized sound as the fallback while a file is still loading.
  3. Keep the spin hum and the hits synthesized, since they react to the game live.
  4. Add a CREDITS.md to the repo listing each file and its source.
