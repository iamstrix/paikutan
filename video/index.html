B. 3 to 5 Minute Gameplay Walkthrough (Pacing: Informative & Clear)

  • 0:00 - 0:45 (Introduction & Setup): Explain the DOST Level Up 3.0 theme (Philippine traditional sports modernized into digital esports). Show the host laptop creating a room and two phones joining via QR/URL.
  • 0:45 - 1:30 (Selection & Stats): Walk through character cosmetics and the 5 mythical trumpos, explaining stat budgets (Strength, Longevity, Stability) and counter cycle.
  • 1:30 - 2:30 (Round 1 Breakdown): Play through Round 1 in full with narration:
    • Paikot phase: How draw speed and accuracy determine initial spin RPM.
    • Bitaw phase: Aim tilt and flick launch timing.
    • Laban phase: Tilt steering, spin friction trade-off, dash cooldown, and boundary safety.
  • 2:30 - 3:30 (Round 2 & Comeback Mechanics): Showcase counterpick phase after Round 1 loss, Agaw-Ikot spin steal on dash collision, and special power execution.
  • 3:30 - 4:30 (Round 3 Decider & Hazards): Show hazards (dog / chicken collisions) altering trajectory, final ring-out or topple, and celebration screen.
  • 4:30 - 5:00 (Tech Architecture & Conclusion): Brief summary of pure web architecture (Three.js 3D physics, Supabase Realtime broadcast synchronization, WebAudio synthesis).
