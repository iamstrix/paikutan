1. Overview & Director Guidelines

This document contains the frame-accurate camera movement directives, rig setups, and cue sheets for filming both the 1-to-2 minute trailer and the 3-to-5 minute gameplay walkthrough.

  • Core Objective: Showcase high-tempo physics clashes while proving zero-install phone controller functionality.
  • Format Requirement: Raw semantic HTML, plain black and white text. Zero CSS, zero JavaScript.

2. In-Engine Camera Presets & Host Shortcuts

Use the local server host session to capture artifact-free footage without network lag.

  • URL: http://localhost:8000/index.html?demo (Autonomous CPU vs CPU match, loops infinitely).
  • URL: http://localhost:8000/index.html?practice (Player vs CPU practice match).
  • URL: http://localhost:8000/index.html?net=local (Local multi-tab multiplayer without internet).
  • Key "H": Toggles Clean Cinematic Mode (hides the entire HUD, overlays, and buttons).
  • Key "C": Cycles spectator camera presets (auto → top → close → orbit).
  • Key "N" / "M": Toggles synthesized WebAudio music / sound effects.

3. Camera Motion Directives & Execution Recipes

A. The Wheel-Height Low Dolly (Chase Rig)

  • Hotkeys: Press C until camera mode is close. Press H for clean mode.
  • Simulated Move: Camera stays 0.35m off the asphalt, tracking the midpoint between both spinning tops. Smoothly creeps forward as they draw close.
  • Director's Cue: Cut right on the spark explosion. Ideal for high-tempo trailer drops.

B. The 360-Degree Barangay Orbit (Crane Rig)

  • Hotkeys: Press C until camera mode is orbit. Press H for clean mode.
  • Simulated Move: Continuous smooth 360-degree rotation at height 4.0m, distance 9.0m around the chalk arena.
  • Director's Cue: Shows the sari-sari store, street props, roaming chickens, and sky lighting. Establishes authentic Filipino culture for theme scoring.

C. The Bird's-Eye Tactical Crane (Overhead Rig)

  • Hotkeys: Press C until camera mode is top.
  • Simulated Move: Fixed overhead crane at height 15m looking straight down into the arena center.
  • Director's Cue: Reveals steering guide arrows, dash trajectory, and edge-tension (labas / ring-out threat). Proves competitive esports fairness.

D. Dual-Device Sync (Physical Over-the-Shoulder Rig)

  • Camera Rig: Physical camera positioned over the players' shoulders.
  • Simulated Move: Slow tilt from player thumbs winding the circle down to the laptop screen showing the trumpo launching.
  • Director's Cue: Visual proof of zero-install phone controller technology.
