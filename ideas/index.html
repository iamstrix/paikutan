Idea 1: "You" / "Ikaw" Trumpo Player Indicator

1. The Problem: Battle Confusion

During fast-paced 1v1 collisions, dashes, and impact screen shakes, players (especially newcomers using phone tilt controls) frequently lose visual track of which trumpo is theirs. This confusion causes players to tilt in the wrong direction, waste their dash, or steer themselves accidentally out of the chalk circle.

2. The Solution: Dynamic Client-Side "YOU" Marker

  • Player Phone Screen: A bold, high-contrast "YOU" (or "IKAW") indicator floats directly above or under the player's own trumpo.
  • Opponent Anonymity: The rival trumpo is simply labeled "RIVAL" or displays their selected trumpo name (e.g., "Tikbalang").
  • Spectator / TV Screen: The big host screen displays neutral tournament tags ("P1" in red, "P2" in blue, or custom player handles).

3. Visual Specification

Indicator Element Visual Placement Behavior & Function
Ground Decal Ring Projected flat on the asphalt directly under the tip. Pulsing circular ring with an embedded forward-facing chevron pointing in the trumpo's current movement direction.
Floating Billboard Tag Hovering 0.35m directly above the crown. Always faces the active camera (billboard orientation) so it never spins or tilts with the top's physical wobble.
Off-Screen Arrow Pointer Screen edge clamp (if camera zooms in tight). If a violent clash throws your trumpo out of the camera's close-up frame, an arrow on the screen edge points toward your off-screen position.
Low Spin Warning State Attached to the text tag. When spin drops below 20%, the "YOU" tag blinks rapidly to alert the player to play defensively or trigger Agaw-Ikot.

4. Implementation Details

  • Engine Hook (js/scene.js): In the render loop, check view === slot. If true, attach the local indicator mesh only to that slot's root group.
  • Zero Host Overhead: The indicator is purely local and client-side; no additional network packets or Supabase synchronization required.

Idea 2: "Sikip Na!" Ring Closing System

1. Competitive Purpose

In street trumpo battles, players sometimes stay along the outer perimeter to stall out an opponent's spin without taking hits. "Sikip Na!" introduces a contracting chalk arena boundary after a set duration, compressing the battleground and forcing an inevitable, decisive clash.

2. Mathematical Parameters

Parameter Value Description
Initial Radius (ARENA_R) 5.0 meters Full chalk arena size at round start.
Squeeze Start (SQUEEZE_T) 20.0 seconds Timestamp when the chalk line begins constricting.
Squeeze Duration (SQUEEZE_DUR) 15.0 seconds Time over which the ring steadily shrinks.
Final Minimum Radius (SQUEEZE_R) 3.0 meters Final tight boundary (playable floor area reduced by 64%).
Elimination Rule Instant Labas (Ring Out) Crossing outside the active shrinking boundary triggers instant round loss.

3. Visual & Audio Telegraphs

  • Warning Phase (t = 17s): Text banner announces "SIKIP NA!" alongside a tricycle horn sound cue.
  • Shrinking Phase (t = 20s to 35s): The 3D chalk circle shifts color from white to warning red as it contracts inward.
  • Tactical Shift: Heavily benefits heavy-knockback trumpos (Tikbalang) and punishes passive evasive play (Diwata).

Idea 3: Character POV Winding & Transparent Spin Overlay (from JMDGG)

1. Concept & Heritage

Adapted from teammate JMDGG's tromPOW repository. Replaces the detached 2D canvas with a diegetic first-person camera looking down through your character's eyes at their hands winding the trumpo.

2. Mechanics & Visual Flow

  • First-Person Camera: Positioned at chest height looking down at the hands holding the lathe-turned trumpo and nylon cord (pisi).
  • Transparent Touch HUD: A semi-transparent circular guide ring (40% to 60% opacity) floats directly over the 3D hands on the smartphone screen.
  • Synchronous Hand Animation: Each circular thumb swipe on the screen animates the character's fingers wrapping another layer of cord around the cone in real time.
  • RPM Feedback: Friction dust and elemental sparks burst from the tip as draw speed approaches maximum RPM.
  • Seamless Transition: When the 3-second winding timer expires, the camera tilts up to follow the physical throw into the 3D chalk circle.

4. Summary of Code Requirements

All three ideas maintain the strict project guidelines:

  • No changes to base physics balance on deadline night.
  • Client-side rendering additions (hands, indicator, chalk scaling) that preserve network synchronization.
  • Full compatibility with DOST Level Up 3.0 theme and esports presentation rubrics.
