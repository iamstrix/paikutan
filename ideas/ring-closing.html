Sikip Na! (Ring Closing Mechanic)

Feature Design Proposal for Paikutan: Trumpo Showdown

Competition: DOST Level Up 3.0 Esports Game Dev Challenge

1. Overview & Competitive Objective

In street trumpo battles, players sometimes stay along the outer perimeter to stall out an opponent's spin without taking hits. "Sikip Na!" introduces a shrinking chalk arena boundary after a set duration, compressing the battleground and forcing an inevitable, high-stakes clash.

  • Eliminates Turtling: Prevents evasive players from running down the round timer.
  • Guaranteed Match Pacing: Guarantees that every round resolves decisively within 35 to 45 seconds, perfectly suited for esports broadcasting.
  • End-Game Climax: Escalates the threat of Labas (Ring Out), making every dash, bump, and collision life-or-death near the closing border.

2. Core Mathematical Specification

Parameter Value Description
Initial Arena Radius (ARENA_R) 5.0 meters The full chalk arena drawn at the start of the round.
Squeeze Start Time (SQUEEZE_T) 20.0 seconds Timestamp when the chalk circle begins actively constricting.
Squeeze Duration (SQUEEZE_DUR) 15.0 seconds Total transition time over which the ring continuously shrinks.
Minimum Final Radius (SQUEEZE_R) 3.0 meters The final tight boundary size (reduces playable floor area by 64%).
Hard Round Cap (MAX_T) 70.0 seconds Absolute match overtime threshold before sudden death spin drain.
Ring Shrink Formula R(t) = max(SQUEEZE_R, ARENA_R - ((t - SQUEEZE_T) / SQUEEZE_DUR) * (ARENA_R - SQUEEZE_R)) Linear interpolation updating the dynamic boundary radius each simulation step.

3. Visual, Audio, & Telegraph System

  1. 3-Second Warning Announcement (at t = 17s):
    • Host HUD Banner: Prominent text banner flashes across the screen: "SIKIP NA!".
    • Audio Cue: Sudden tricycle horn or barangay whistle sound effect signals the impending shrink.
    • Chalk Flash: The white chalk perimeter pulses between white (#fbf6ea) and warning orange.
  2. Active Constriction Phase (t = 20s to 35s):
    • Chalk Retraction: The visible 3D chalk ring shifts from white to hot danger red (#ff8a7a) as it scales down.
    • Ground Dust Effect: Fine chalk dust particles scrape inward along the asphalt as the border recedes.
    • Throw Lines Recede: The player launch lines and floor markings contract proportionately.
  3. Final Sudden Death Perimeter (t > 35s):
    • The ring locks at a tight 3.0-meter diameter.
    • Any trumpo whose center of mass exceeds R(t) immediately triggers the Labas (Ring Out) elimination flag.

4. Impact on Trumpo Archetypes & Strategy

Trumpo Role / Affinity Tactical Impact Under "Sikip Na!"
Tikbalang Heavy Striker (Stampede) Major Beneficiary: A tighter ring makes Tikbalang's 2-second Stampede and terminal hoof-stomp almost impossible to dodge, easily shoving opponents across the short distance into Labas.
Agimat Defensive Anchor (Shield) High Priority Defense: When space runs out, collisions become inevitable. Agimat's 70% knockback reduction allows it to absorb heavy dashes without flying out of the ring.
Kidlat Speed / Burst (Lightning) High Risk, High Reward: High speed becomes dangerous near red chalk boundaries. Players must execute precise tilt counter-steering to land the lightning bolt without skidding out.
Bakunawa Disruptor (Eclipse) Lethal Punisher: Casting Eclipse in a 3-meter circle is devastating; reversed controls guarantee that a panicked rival steers themselves directly over the closing chalk line.
Diwata Regenerator (Blessing) Skill Check: Can no longer run away to freely restore spin; must time blessing between forced clashes and use Agaw-Ikot dash comebacks to survive.

5. Implementation Architecture & Code Touchpoints

The game architecture already supports dynamic arena radius tracking across client-server snapshots:

  • Simulation Engine (js/sim.js):
    • w.ring dynamically computes current chalk radius based on w.t, SQUEEZE_T, and SQUEEZE_DUR.
    • Ring-out boundary check evaluates Math.hypot(tp.x, tp.z) > w.ring rather than static ARENA_R.
    • Snapshot serializes s.ring so phone clients mirror the exact boundary.
  • 3D Scene Renderer (js/scene.js):
    • Chalk mesh scaling: chalk.scale.setScalar(ringR / ARENA_R).
    • Chalk dynamic tint: chalk.material.color.set(ringR < ARENA_R - 0.01 ? '#ff8a7a' : '#fbf6ea').
  • Spectator & Phone HUDs (js/host.js & js/play.js):
    • Display on-screen banner countdown when w.ring < ARENA_R.
    • Phone mini-radar scales the circle graphic down synchronously with the host screen.

6. Conclusion & Esports Viability

"Sikip Na!" bridges traditional Filipino play with modern battle royale design patterns. It respects cultural play (the chalk circle is standard in street games) while solving the single biggest threat to spectator engagement: late-round stagnation.
