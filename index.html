How to play
  • WASD walk, Shift run (bump someone and they tumble!)
  • Step inside the ring of stones to start a match
  • Spin: draw fast circles on the ring for 3 seconds: speed = power, neatness = steadiness
  • Throw: drag the mouse up and let go (Space = medium throw)
  • Fight: WASD steers, Space / click = PALO dash-strike (hits steal spin), E / right-click = power
  • Knock someone out = +1, last trompo spinning = +3. Out? Click the circle to flick your tsinelas!
  • Last trompo spinning in the circle wins. 1–6 to taunt! 📱 uses your phone as the trompo.
