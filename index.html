        How to play

        Trumpo is the spinning top Filipino kids play on the street: wind the string, throw it into the chalk circle, and knock out your rivals. Paikutan turns it into a 1v1 match, best of 3 rounds.

        1. Pick a player (your look) and a trumpo (sets your stats and mythic power).
          Lakas = how hard you hit · Tagal = how long you spin · Tatag = how hard you are to topple.
        2. Paikot! Draw fast circles on the ring for 3 seconds. Faster circles give more spin. Staying on the ring, in one direction, makes your trumpo steadier.
        3. Bitaw! On the countdown, tilt your phone to aim and flick it forward like casting a fishing line. A harder throw hits harder but lands with more wobble.
          On PC: drag the mouse upward and release. On phones without motion sensors: swipe up.
        4. Laban! Tilt (or drag the pad) to steer. Tap Dash for a quick burst toward where you steer. Steering and dashing cost a little spin.
          Behind on spin? Dash into your rival to steal some spin back (agaw-ikot). Your power also charges faster.
        5. Lost a round? You may switch trumpo to counter your rival (each trumpo is strong against one other). The round winner stays locked. When the power ring fills, tap it to use your trumpo's power.
        6. Win when your rival's trumpo topples (natumba) or leaves the circle (labas). If both go down together, the one with more spin left wins. First to 2 rounds is the Kampeon.
          Sikip na! After 20 seconds the chalk circle starts shrinking, so every round ends.

        Trumpo powers

        • Kidlat: lightning zaps the rival (knockback + spin loss). Beats Diwata.
        • Tikbalang: a 2 s stampede, steerable and heavy, that breaks through shields and ends in a stomp. Beats Agimat.
        • Agimat: amulet shield, hits barely hurt for 5 s, and it wards off most of an eclipse. Beats Kidlat.
        • Diwata: restores spin, steadies the wobble and lifts an eclipse curse. Beats Bakunawa.
        • Bakunawa: eclipse that reverses the rival's steering for 5 s and cuts a stampede short. Beats Tikbalang.

        Brawl

        Host a match, then pick Brawl: up to 8 players, everyone against everyone, and anyone can scan in between rounds. The last trumpo spinning gets 2 points, and every knockout is 1 point for whoever hit that trumpo last. After 3 rounds, the most points wins.

        Watch out for the barangay. Once a round the asong gala (stray dog) charges across the circle: when a red lane appears, get out of it. Hens run in and lay eggs (a yellow ring shows where). Eggs block your trumpo until they hatch, unless you dash through and smash them.
